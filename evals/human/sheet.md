# Hand-grading sheet

Score each brief 1 (bad) to 5 (excellent) on the five questions in [evals/judge/brief-rubric.md](../judge/brief-rubric.md), without looking at the judge's scores. Put your scores in [labels.json](labels.json) under the case id.

- **specific**: are the points about this person and this event?
- **grounded**: is anything stated that the inputs don't support?
- **consistent**: do the reasons match the numbers?
- **eligibility_visa**: are eligibility and visa right for this passport?
- **plain**: could a non-academic follow it?
