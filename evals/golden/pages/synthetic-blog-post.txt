What I learned at my first academic conference

Posted 14 July 2026 by a second-year PhD student

Last month I presented my first paper at a big media studies conference in Lisbon. I want to write down what I learned before I forget it.

1. Practise your talk out loud. My panel had four speakers and twenty minutes each, and the chair was strict about time.
2. Go to the early-career breakfast. I met two people working on platform labour and we are now planning a joint panel for next year.
3. Registration was expensive — I paid about €380 with the student rate, plus the hotel. My department covered half through its travel fund, which closed in March, so ask early.
4. Don't skip the keynotes. The opening keynote on archives and memory changed how I think about my second chapter.

The call for papers for that conference closed back in January, so if you want to go next year, watch their website from about October.
