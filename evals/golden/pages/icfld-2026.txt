Call for Papers: 4th International Conference and Fair on Land and Development (ICFLD 2026)

By CSHD UNILAG
July 6, 2026 August 31, 2026

Theme: Leveraging Digital Technologies for Sustainable Land Governance, Smart Urban Management, and Inclusive Housing Delivery in Africa

Conference Dates: Tuesday, 24 – Wednesday, 25 November 2026

Venue: Faculty of Education Glasshouse Hall, University of Lagos, Akoka-Yaba, Lagos, Nigeria

The ARUA Centre of Excellence for Urbanization and Habitable Cities (CoE-UHC) invites researchers, academics, policymakers, built environment professionals, development partners, civil society organisations, technology innovators, students, and practitioners to submit abstracts for the 4th International Conference and Fair on Land and Development (ICFLD 2026).

As Africa experiences the fastest rate of urbanisation globally, cities continue to face increasing pressure on land governance, housing delivery, infrastructure, environmental sustainability, and climate resilience. At the same time, emerging digital technologies—including Artificial Intelligence (AI), Geographic Information Systems (GIS), blockchain, Building Information Modelling (BIM), Digital Twins, remote sensing, digital cadastre, and PropTech—are creating new opportunities to transform urban development across the continent.

ICFLD 2026 will provide a multidisciplinary platform for exchanging knowledge, presenting innovative research, showcasing digital solutions, and fostering collaborations that promote sustainable land governance, smart urban management, and inclusive housing delivery throughout Africa.

Conference Aim

The conference aims to advance knowledge, strengthen partnerships, and promote evidence-based policies and innovative digital solutions for sustainable, inclusive, and resilient land governance, smart urban management, and housing delivery across Africa.

Conference Objectives

The conference seeks to:

• Examine opportunities and challenges presented by emerging digital technologies such as AI, GIS, blockchain, Digital Twins, BIM, digital cadastre, remote sensing, and PropTech.

• Showcase innovative research, technological solutions, and best practices that improve land administration, housing delivery, tenure security, climate resilience, and environmental sustainability.

• Foster collaboration among governments, academia, industry, development partners, professional institutions, and civil society.

• Explore legal, ethical, governance, and policy frameworks that support inclusive digital transformation.

• Generate evidence-based policy recommendations that contribute to:

• African Union Agenda 2063

• Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

• New Urban Agenda

• Voluntary Guidelines on the Responsible Governance of Tenure (VGGT)

Conference Sub-Themes

Researchers are invited to submit papers under, but not limited to, the following areas:

• Digital Technologies in Land Governance and Administration

• Smart Cities, Urban Management and Digital Innovation

• Artificial Intelligence, Big Data and Urban Analytics

• Digital Cadastre, GIS, Remote Sensing and Spatial Data Infrastructure

• Housing Finance, PropTech and Inclusive Housing Delivery

• Cooperative Housing and Community-led Development

• Land Tenure Security, Digital Rights and Land Conflict Management

• Climate Change, Urban Resilience and Environmental Sustainability

• Governance, Policy Innovation and Digital Transformation

• Gender-responsive, Inclusive and Accessible Land and Housing Systems

• Informal Settlements Upgrading and Digital Inclusion

• Land Value Capture, Municipal Finance and Urban Infrastructure

• Digital Twins, BIM and Smart Infrastructure

• Citizen Science, Participatory Planning and Community Engagement

Special Focus Sessions

ICFLD 2026 will feature dedicated sessions including:

Lagos Digital Land Governance Showcase

A showcase of innovative digital land administration systems, including e-GIS, digital building approval systems, and smart governance initiatives with lessons applicable across Africa.

Doctoral and Early-Career Researchers Forum

A mentorship, networking, and peer-review platform designed specifically for postgraduate students and emerging researchers.

Abstract Submission Guidelines

Abstracts should include:

• Paper title

• Author(s)’ names (Surname in CAPITAL LETTERS)

• Institutional affiliation

• Email address(es)

• Telephone number(s)

• Clearly identified corresponding author

• Five (5) keywords

Formatting Requirements

• Maximum 250 words

• Times New Roman

• Font size 12

Submit your abstract here: https://forms.gle/5281ewL7aKXBkVzN8

Publication Opportunity

All submitted abstracts and full papers will undergo a double-blind peer-review process.

Selected high-quality papers will be considered for publication in the African Journal of Housing and Sustainable Development , subject to the journal’s editorial and peer-review requirements.
