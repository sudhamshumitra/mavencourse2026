IAMCR 2027 to be held in São Paulo, Brazil

Claudia Lago, Chair of the 2027 São Paulo Local Commission and Daya Thussu, IAMCR President, at IAMCR 2026

We are pleased to announce that IAMCR will hold its 2027 annual conference in São Paulo, Brazil, bringing the conference back to South America after ten years and marking the Association's 70th anniversary.

Hosted by the School of Communications and Arts at the University of São Paulo (ECA/USP) in partnership with the Associação de Apoio à Arte e Comunicação (ARCO), the conference will take place from 18 to 22 July 2027 under the theme Multilateralism, Democracy, Diversity: Communication for a More Just World .

Watch this short video for a glimpse of what awaits in São Paulo next year.

The decision to hold the conference in São Paulo was confirmed with the signing of an agreement during the IAMCR 2026 conference in Galway and was formally announced at the closing ceremony on 2 July.

Claudia Lago, Chair of the Local Organising Commission, said:

We are very pleased to host IAMCR 2027. We are confident that everyone who attends will enjoy not only the academic debates and activities, but also the welcoming atmosphere here in Brazil.

The conference is expected to bring together around 1,500 media and communication researchers from around the world. The call for proposals will open in November 2026. Stay tuned!

About the host

Founded in 1934, University of São Paulo (USP) is the largest public state university in Brazil. A leader in teaching, research, and innovation, it consistently tops the major international rankings in Latin America, including Times Higher Education (THE), QS, and SCImago. USP is located in eight cities across the state of São Paulo, including its capital, São Paulo, a prominent cultural and intellectual center of Latin America.

The School of Communications and Arts (ECA) was founded in 1966 and is recognised for the excellence of its teaching, research, and academic production in the fields of Communication, Arts, and Information. It is also widely respected for the quality of its undergraduate and graduate programmes.
