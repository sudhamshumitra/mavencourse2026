54th Annual Conference on
South Asia

Join us October 28-31, 2026!

The 2026 conference theme is Transcending Borders and Nationalism – South Asia as cultures, histories, and topography

Our submission portal is now closed. We are no longer accepting proposals for 2026. The 2027 submission portal will open on February 1.

View the 54th ACSA Conference Program Book

The Annual Conference on South Asia invites scholars, students, and professionals to Madison, Wisconsin, for a four-day event featuring research panels and round tables, lectures, performances, film screenings, booksellers, association receptions, and other special presentations! The conference has grown year-by-year since its inception in 1971 and welcomed over 1,000 registered attendees in 2025.

The 54th Conference in 2026 is scheduled to meet in-person at
The Madison Concourse Hotel & Governor’s Club (1 W. Dayton, Madison, WI 53703).

Questions? Email us at conference@southasia.wisc.edu

ACSA-related inquiries should be sent only to the email address listed above. Inquiries sent to conference staff on alternate accounts will not be answered.

When are the Proposal deadlines?

Symposium Proposals are due March 1st; Panels, Single Papers, and Round Table Proposals are due April 5th. Film submissions are due April 5th via email.

What is the last date to register online?

The last day to register online is October 10th. In person attendees may register upon arrival if unable to register online.

How much are the registration fees?

Early bird registration is open until October 1st.

Non student- $300

Student- $150

Emeritus- $150

After October 1st, regular registration fees applies

Non student- $310

Student- $160

Emeritus- $160

When does the registration portal open?

The portal opens on February 1st

I would like to submit a proposal but am unable to pay online via credit card. What can I do?

You may select the option to pay by check and send us an email to confirm your registration.

Where can I find my registration receipt?

On the website, navigate to “My Profile” under login and register .

Select “Download Receipt” in the blue box

When can I request a registration fee waiver?

The fee waiver request form opens in August. Please ensure you are receiving conference email announcements for the most accurate deadlines and information on fee waivers.

How many sessions can I participate in?

All attendees can participate in a maximum of two sessions Thursday through Saturday in any role (presenter, organizer, chair, discussant, or some combination of these roles). Symposia are separate and are not included in this limit.
