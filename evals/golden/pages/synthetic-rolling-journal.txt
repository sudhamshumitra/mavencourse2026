Open Call for Submissions

Journal of Southern Media Histories (peer-reviewed, open access, published by an independent scholarly collective)

We accept article submissions on a rolling basis throughout the year. There is no deadline: articles are reviewed as they arrive and published online as soon as they are accepted.

Scope: histories of radio, film, print and digital media across Africa, Asia, Latin America and the Middle East.

Length: 7,000–9,000 words including notes.

There are no article processing charges (APCs) and no submission fees.

Submit through our online system after creating an author account.
