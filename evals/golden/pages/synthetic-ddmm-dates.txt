International Workshop on Colonial Archives 2027

Hosted by the Institute for Global History, Example University, Germany. All dates on this page are written day/month/year (DD/MM/YYYY).

Workshop dates: 08/06/2027 – 10/06/2027 (in person, Göttingen)

Deadlines
- Abstract submission (max. 300 words): 03/02/2027
- Notification of acceptance: 01/03/2027
- Full paper for pre-circulation: 05/05/2027

There is no registration fee. Accommodation for three nights is covered for all accepted presenters; travel costs can be partly reimbursed (up to €400) for early-career researchers without institutional funding.

Please send your abstract to workshop2027@example-uni.de.
