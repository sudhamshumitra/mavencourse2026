European Network for Area Studies – Congress 2027
Dublin, Ireland, 7–10 September 2027 (hybrid)

Call for papers
Paper and panel proposals are due 28 February 2027.

Registration fees
Because we have members in three currency zones, fees can be paid in euro, pounds or US dollars:
• Standard delegate: €320 (or £280, or US$350)
• Student delegate: €140 (or £120, or US$150)
• Online participation, any status: €60

Fee waivers: ten fee waivers are reserved for presenters based in low- and middle-income countries. Tick the box when you submit your proposal.
